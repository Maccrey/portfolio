About Maccrey

A developer who takes problems from idea to product

Since starting to code at 50, I have turned ideas into usable services and learned through planning, implementation, launch, and iteration.

HOW I WORK

Understand the user's problem before choosing technology

I break requirements into small flows, build something usable quickly, and revisit problems found after launch. Accessibility, privacy, and operability are part of the initial design.

WHAT I BRING

Ownership from first idea through ongoing product work

  • Mobile: Flutter apps, Firebase integration, authentication, and data flows
  • Web: Product web apps, multilingual sites, static deployment, and SEO
  • Problem solving: Documenting root causes and verification to prevent repeat failures
  • Collaboration: Turning unclear requests into small decisions and reviewable outcomes

CURRENT BUILD · FLUTTER · FIREBASE · AI

KINORA — family care that starts with conversation

KINORA helps older adults reflect on everyday life—meals, hydration, medication, sleep, pain, and mood—through natural conversations with an AI character. Family members can follow relevant changes and check in. The goal is to support peace of mind while preserving independence, not to increase monitoring.

01 · SENIOR APP

A conversation worth returning to

The senior app makes it easy to share how the day is going and continue with context from earlier conversations.

02 · FAMILY EXPERIENCE

Share only the changes that matter

Rather than expose every conversation, the family experience shares useful check-in summaries within the bounds of consent and relationships.

03 · PRODUCT ENGINEERING

Design the product and its safeguards together

Alongside Flutter and Firebase implementation, I review conversation memory, data minimization, consent, notification accuracy, and failure cases.
